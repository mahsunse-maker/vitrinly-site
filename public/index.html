Questions

Does Vitrinly post to my status automatically?

No app is allowed to post to your status without you. Vitrinly prepares everything and opens the share sheet — you just pick "My status".

Where are my listings stored?

Only on your phone. Use "Back up" to save a copy to your own Drive, iCloud or Files, and restore it on a new phone.

Does the AI keep my photos?

No. When you use "Prepare with AI", the photos and text of that listing are sent to create the design and are not stored by Vitrinly. Details are in the privacy policy.

Can the AI change my price or phone number?

No. Price, phone, business name and your description are always shown exactly as you typed them. The AI decides the design and writes the title, short highlights and the video script. You review it before sharing.

How long can videos be?

Up to 60 seconds per status. For longer videos you choose which 60 seconds to share.

Which languages are supported?

English, Turkish, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Russian and Arabic.
